# Brief (what the video was supposed to be)

A 20 second product-launch film for crewboard, a free open-source local web page that shows every Claude Code
session and its agents live, a task list that you and your agents both write, and a Now page where agents leave
questions for you. The benchmark is a launch film by Apple, Linear, Raycast or Arc: it should look made by a design
studio, not like a screen recording or a template.

- The only product UI shown is crewboard's real page (example data). Nothing else may be presented as product UI.
- The page is lit like an object on a designed background with depth. The camera pushes in on one feature at a time.
- Features, in order: every session, live; its agents too; what each one is doing; one list for you and your agents;
  answer what's waiting; runs on your machine.
- One short sentence of type per shot. Eased motion, one motion at a time. Cuts on the beat.
- End card: the name crewboard, one line, and github.com/VyasSathya/crewboard.
- Music only, no voiceover. Faceless. Two formats: 16:9 (1920x1080) and a 9:16 recomposition (1080x1920).
- Colours and fonts come from the product's own theme: neutral greys, Geist and Geist Mono; status colours amber
  (working / in progress), violet (waiting on you), green (done), in a dark and a light theme.
