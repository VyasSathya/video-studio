# Brief (pairwise)

Two short, faceless product-launch films, each for a different free developer tool, each shown as frame strips
(one frame every 0.5 s, time on each tile; a 16:9 strip and a 9:16 strip per film). The benchmark for both is a
launch film by Apple, Linear, Raycast or Arc: it should look made by a design studio, not like a screen recording
or a template.

Rules both films had to follow: only the product's real UI, in its own colours and fonts; the product lit like an
object on a designed background; the camera does the transitions and pushes in on one feature at a time; one motion
at a time, eased; one short sentence of type per shot; no generated people, no voiceover; an end card with the
name, one line and the URL; a 9:16 recomposition, not a crop.
