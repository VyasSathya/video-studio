# Rubric (answer every item YES or NO, with the frame time that decides it)

1. Studio look: at a glance it reads as a designed launch film (Apple / Linear / Raycast / Arc level), not a screen
   recording, slideshow or template.
2. Real UI only: one product UI appears, it looks like a real shipped app, and no invented screens, icons or data
   are presented as the product.
3. Depth: the product sits on a designed background with depth (light, shadow, falloff), not flat on a flat colour.
4. One feature at a time: each shot is clearly about one thing, and the camera frames that thing.
5. Story: from the frames alone, a viewer gets all four features: always listening; it talks back; say "wait" and it
   stops; fully local.
6. Type: each feature has a few words of type, legible at phone size, never covering UI text and never cropped.
7. Typography: one sans plus at most one mono; sizes, weights and alignment are consistent across shots.
8. Restraint: neutral palette with one or two accents; no over-saturation, glowing halos, generic particles, robots or
   brains, or a centered title on a gradient as the opening.
9. Edges: no UI text or caption is cut mid-word by the frame edge or hidden behind other elements (the panel body
   bleeding off frame is fine).
10. Consistency: nothing on screen contradicts the type shown with it.
11. Pacing: shot lengths vary; no shot looks like it flashes by too fast to read or overstays.
12. End card: name, one line and URL, clean, centered, legible, nothing else competing.
13. No AI tells: no garbled text, warped shapes, generated people, faces or hands.
14. 9:16: recomposed for the tall frame, not a blind crop; type and the framed UI fit with margins.
15. Overall: you would post this as the product's launch video without changes.
