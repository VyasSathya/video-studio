# Motion showreel (not yet run)

Built on the launch engine: `launch.render(brief, out, w, h)` with many features and short holds.
- One feature per product, each 2 beats at 120 bpm (1 s) with the camera already moving; titles of 1 or 2
  words. Arc-style word cards (1 or 2 words, all caps, 14 to 18 percent of frame height, brand color, about 1 s)
  may close the run, never mixed with sentence titles.
- Every shot a real capture (`capture.mjs`); each product's own tokens on its shots; the reel's own mark on the
  end card.
- Verify like a launch film (skills/product-launch rubric, blind judge) before anyone sees it.
